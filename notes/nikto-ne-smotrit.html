When nobody is looking

11 August 2026 · 2 min read

The repository has zero stars. Nobody opened the article. The question of what for shows up in the second month.

An audience is bad fuel, because it is not under your control and it arrives years late, if it arrives at all. Work that runs on views ends exactly when the views do.

What is left is what can be checked today: is it clearer now, does the thing work that did not work yesterday.

That sounds like consolation. It is not consolation, it is the only report available.
