Comparison

21 July 2026 · 2 min read

People compare themselves not to their earlier selves but to the best visible result.

At work this looks reasonable: there are people who do it better. But what is visible is not a random sample. You see the releases, not the months when nothing shipped. The report, not the draft.

A leaderboard is built so that you never come first: someone is always above, and there is one place at the top.

There is one reference point that works — your own state six months ago. It is dull and gives no reason for pride. It does, however, exist.
