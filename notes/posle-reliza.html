The emptiness after a release

24 August 2026 · 2 min read

You push the tag, CI goes green, the package lands on PyPI. By evening, nothing.

This is not ingratitude and not burnout. While the goal stood, it held the schedule: it was clear what to do on Saturday and what to think about while standing in a queue. The goal went, and structure went with it — not joy. Joy, if there was any, lasted forty minutes.

The usual explanation is that it must have been the wrong project. It was the right project. Achievement does not produce meaning; it spends it.

Hence the habit of starting the next thing immediately. It works, and it is also why you never stop.
