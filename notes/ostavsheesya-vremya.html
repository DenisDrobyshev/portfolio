The time that is left

18 August 2026 · 2 min read

Counted in years, there are plenty. Counted in weeks, they fit on one screen.

Thirty years is about fifteen hundred Saturdays. Not few, not many: a finite number you can see all at once.

People usually turn this into a call to hurry. I think it is the opposite. Knowing the count does not speed you up, it reorders things: it becomes visible that the list is not merely long, it is too long for that number, and some of it will never be done.

The choice gets made either way. The only question is whether you make it or it is made for you, by default.
